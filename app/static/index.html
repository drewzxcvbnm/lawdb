Search tips

Use Latvian words from the decision. Accents are optional; word endings are matched as written. Put a phrase in quotation marks, use OR for alternatives, or -word to exclude a word. Identifier mode matches a complete case number, application number, ECLI, or document UUID exactly. Queries are not translated.
